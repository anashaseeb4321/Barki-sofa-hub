Why buy a made-to-order u-shape sofas?

High-street retailers sell fixed sizes from a catalogue. Each sofa is made to order after you order it, which means the dimensions match your room rather than a warehouse template — and because there's no showroom overhead in the price, a made-to-order sofa from us typically costs less than a mass-produced equivalent.

Order entirely over WhatsApp: send a photo of your space and rough measurements, and we'll come back with fabric options and a firm price. Free delivery anywhere in mainland UK, usually within 2–3 days, with cash on delivery available.

We do not sell a separate manufacturer's warranty.
