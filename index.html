Good to know

Questions, answered

How long does delivery take?

Every sofa is made to order and delivered free across mainland UK within 2–3 days. We'll confirm a date once your order is placed.

Can I choose a custom size?

Yes. Tell us your room dimensions on WhatsApp and we'll tailor the length, depth and configuration to fit.

What payment options do you offer?

Cash on delivery, every time. There's no deposit and nothing to pay up front — you settle in full when your sofa arrives at your door.

Do you offer fabric samples?

We can send you our full colour card and swatch details over WhatsApp before you commit to an order.

What happens if something goes wrong with it?

We do not sell a separate manufacturer's warranty. What you have instead is your statutory protection under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, which we honour in full: if a sofa is faulty, not of satisfactory quality or not as described, we repair, replace or refund it, and we arrange and pay for collection. Full detail is on our returns page.
