About the Chesterfield 3 Seater Pull-Out Sofa Bed

This is a Chesterfield 3 seater with the traditional British pull-out bed. Take off the seat cushions, pull the strap, and a metal frame with its own mattress unfolds out of the seat to make a proper bed. When you are done, it folds back inside and the sofa looks just as it did.

On the outside it is deep-buttoned velvet with crystal buttons, curved scroll arms and chrome trim. Because the mattress is separate from the seat cushions, this is a good choice if guests stay over often. It is also known as a Chesterfield fold-out sofa bed, metal action sofa bed or bed settee.

Every one is made to order, so you choose the fabric and the colour. Frames are solid hardwood. We deliver free across mainland UK, usually within two to three days of your order being confirmed, and you pay when it arrives — see delivery for the detail, including the access measurements worth checking first. Because each sofa is made to your order, please read our returns and cancellations policy before you order.

Common questions

What kind of bed is it?

A traditional pull-out bed: a fold-out metal frame with a separate mattress, stored inside the seat. You remove the seat cushions and pull it out. The videos on this page show it opening and closing.

How is this different from your other Chesterfield sofa beds?

Our other Chesterfield sofa beds have a seat that slides forward to make the bed, with no separate mattress. This one has its own mattress on a metal frame, which many people prefer for more regular guests.

Is the mattress included?

Yes. The mattress comes with the sofa bed and folds away inside it.

Can I have it in a different colour?

Yes. It is made to order in any of our 93 colours across 8 fabrics. Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you choose.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. There's no deposit and nothing to pay up front. You pay when it arrives and you have checked it, by cash or bank transfer. Full details are on our delivery page.

Do you offer finance or monthly payments?

We don't offer finance, so there are no credit checks, no interest and no monthly bills. Instead, you pay nothing up front: no deposit and nothing when you order. You pay in full only when your order arrives at your door and you have checked it, by cash or bank transfer.
