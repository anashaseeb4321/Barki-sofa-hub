About the Meralane 2+3 Seater Sofa

Every Meralane 2+3 Seater Sofa is made to order. You choose the fabric, the colour and the exact dimensions of both pieces, and they do not have to be a standard pair. If one wall is shorter than the other, we size each piece to the wall it will actually stand against.

Frames are solid hardwood with foam and fibre cushioning. Every sofa is covered by your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, so if one turns out to be faulty we repair, replace or refund it and collect at our cost. Both pieces are made together from the same fabric roll, which is the surest way to avoid the slight shade difference that can appear between production batches if you buy them months apart. We deliver free across mainland UK, usually within two to three days of your order being confirmed, and cash on delivery is available so you can pay when it arrives — see delivery for the detail, including the access measurements worth checking first. Because each sofa is cut to your specification, please read our returns and cancellations policy before you order.

Common questions

Can I have the Meralane 2+3 Seater Sofa in a custom size?

Yes. Send us your room dimensions on WhatsApp and we'll adjust the length, depth and configuration to fit. There's no extra charge for standard resizing.

Can I order just the 3-seater, or just the 2-seater?

Yes. The set price covers both pieces, but either can be bought on its own and we will quote for it. Some customers buy the three-seater first and add the two-seater later, which is fine — just tell us, and we will keep a note of the exact fabric and colour so the second piece matches.

Do both pieces have to be the same fabric?

No. Most people match them, but a contrasting two-seater is a perfectly good look and costs the same. If you are matching later rather than ordering together, be aware that fabric batches can vary slightly in shade.

How much space does a 2+3 set need?

Measure both walls the pieces will sit against, not just the longer one, and remember to leave walking room between them. Send us both measurements and we will size the two pieces to suit rather than handing you a fixed pair that almost fits.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available on most orders, or you can pay a deposit and settle the balance when the sofa arrives. Full details are on our delivery page.
