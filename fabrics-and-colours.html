Fabric questions

Can I see the fabric before I order?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send close-up photos of the fabric and colour you like, and we can arrange for you to see a sample in person if the exact shade matters to you.

Can I have any of these colours on any sofa?

Tell us which sofa you like and the colour you've chosen, and we'll confirm it's available for that sofa before anything is made.

Does the fabric change the price?

Your final price depends on the sofa, the size and the fabric you choose. We confirm your exact price on WhatsApp before we start, so there are no surprises.

Why does a colour look different on my screen?

Every screen shows colour slightly differently, so the real fabric can look a little lighter or darker than the photo. If the exact shade matters, ask us for a close-up photo or a sample.

Want to compare how each fabric wears? Read our sofa fabric guide.

Found a colour you love?

Send us the colour code and your room measurements on WhatsApp and we'll come back with sizing help and a firm price — usually within minutes.
