About the Ambassador Sofa

Every Ambassador Sofa we build is made to order in our Blackburn workshop. You choose the fabric, the colour and the exact dimensions — so if your alcove is 20cm narrower than a standard sofa, we build it 20cm narrower rather than asking you to compromise.

Frames are solid hardwood and the seats are built from foam with a fibre layer over the top, which is what stops a sofa flattening in the spot everyone sits in. Every piece carries a manufacturer's guarantee, and if you would prefer firmer or softer seating we will adjust the fill before we build. We deliver free across mainland UK, usually within two to three days of your order being confirmed, and cash on delivery is available so you can pay when it arrives — see delivery for the detail, including the access measurements worth checking first. Because each sofa is cut to your specification, please read our returns and cancellations policy before you order.

Common questions

Can I have the Ambassador Sofa in a custom size?

Yes. Send us your room dimensions on WhatsApp and we'll adjust the length, depth and configuration to fit. There's no extra charge for standard resizing.

Can I see the fabric before I order?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send close-up photographs of the fabric you are considering in the colour you want, and we can arrange for you to see a sample in person if the exact shade matters to you.

What is included in the price?

The sofa built to your specification, in the fabric and colour you choose, plus free delivery to mainland UK. There is no separate charge for standard resizing and no delivery surcharge for larger pieces. The price we confirm on WhatsApp is the price you pay.

How firm are the seats?

Seats are built from foam with a fibre layer over the top, which gives support underneath without feeling hard to sit on. If you have a strong preference for firmer or softer seating, tell us when you order and we will adjust the fill.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available on most orders, or you can pay a deposit and settle the balance when the sofa arrives. Full details are on our delivery page.
