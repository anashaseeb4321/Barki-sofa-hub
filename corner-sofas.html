Why buy a made-to-order corner sofas?

High-street retailers sell fixed sizes from a catalogue. Each sofa is made to order after you order it, which means the dimensions match your room rather than a warehouse template — and because there's no showroom overhead in the price, a made-to-order sofa from us typically costs less than a mass-produced equivalent.

Order entirely over WhatsApp: send a photo of your space and rough measurements, and we'll come back with fabric options and a firm price. Free delivery anywhere in mainland UK, usually within 2–3 days, with cash on delivery available.

Corner Sofas — your questions

How much does a corner sofa cost?

Our corner sofas start at £599, including free UK delivery. The final price depends on the size and fabric you choose — message us with your dimensions for an exact quote.

Do you deliver to my area?

We deliver free across mainland UK, including England, Wales and most of Scotland. Message us with your postcode to confirm.

Can I see the fabric before I order?

Yes — we can send you our full colour card and swatch details over WhatsApp before you commit.

What happens if something goes wrong with it?

We do not sell a separate manufacturer's warranty. What you have instead is your statutory protection under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, which we honour in full: if a sofa is faulty, not of satisfactory quality or not as described, we repair, replace or refund it, and we arrange and pay for collection. Full detail is on our returns page.
