Why order a made-to-order sofa?

A made-to-order sofa is produced after you order it, so it fits your room instead of a standard showroom size. If you need a corner suite 15cm shorter to clear a radiator, or a left-hand chaise instead of right, tell us and we arrange that change for you — not through a call centre.

It also means no showroom rent built into your price. Most orders are delivered within 2–3 days of confirming your fabric.
