Why buy locally made?

Buying from a Blackburn workshop rather than a national chain means your sofa is built by the people you're actually speaking to. If you need a corner suite 15cm shorter to clear a radiator, or a left-hand chaise instead of right, we make that change on the bench — not through a call centre.

It also means no showroom rent built into your price, and no six-week wait. Most orders are built and delivered within 2–3 days.
