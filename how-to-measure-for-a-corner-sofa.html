How to Measure Your Room for a Corner Sofa

Getting the measurements right is the difference between a corner sofa that transforms a room and one that never quite fits. Here's exactly what to measure, in the order we ask for it.

What you'll need

A tape measure, a pen and paper, and about ten minutes. If you can, get a second person to hold the other end of the tape — measuring a long wall alone is where most mistakes creep in.

Step 1 — Measure the walls the sofa will sit against

A corner sofa occupies two walls. Measure each wall separately, from corner to corner, at floor level rather than at waist height — skirting boards, pipe boxing and uneven plaster all change the usable length.

Write down both numbers. We'll refer to these as your long side and short side.

Leave a gap. Don't order a sofa that exactly fills the wall. Leave at least 5–10cm at each end so the sofa can be positioned without scuffing the wall, and so skirting boards don't force it forward.

Step 2 — Check what's on those walls

Note the position and height of anything that stops the sofa sitting flush:

  • Radiators — measure from the floor to the bottom of the radiator, and how far it projects from the wall
  • Plug sockets — a sofa back covering a socket is a common regret
  • Window sills — a low sill may limit the back height of the sofa
  • Doors — check which way they swing into the room

Step 3 — Measure the access route (the step people skip)

This is the single most common cause of a failed delivery. Your sofa has to physically get into the room. Measure:

PointWhat to measure
Front doorWidth and height of the opening, with the door open
HallwayNarrowest width, and the width at any turn
StairsWidth, plus the ceiling height above the turn if there is one
Room doorwayWidth and height of the opening
Tightest cornerDiagonal space available to pivot

If any of these are tight, tell us before you order. We can often build the sofa in modular sections that fit through a narrow door and assemble in the room.

Step 4 — Think about how you'll use it

Measurements tell us what fits. How you sit tells us what to recommend. Let us know:

  • How many people usually sit on it at once
  • Whether anyone lies full-length on it (this decides the chaise length)
  • Whether you prefer a firmer, upright seat or a deep sink-in one
  • Whether the chaise should be on the left or right as you face the sofa

Step 5 — Send it to us

Send the numbers on WhatsApp along with a photo or two of the room. A photo tells us things measurements don't — the light, the doorway position, where the TV is — and lets us suggest a configuration that actually works.

We'll come back with the exact dimensions we'd build, fabric options and a firm price, usually within minutes.

A quick checklist

  • Long wall length (at floor level)
  • Short wall length (at floor level)
  • Radiator, socket and sill positions
  • Front door, hallway, stairs and room door widths
  • Left-hand or right-hand chaise
  • Photos of the room
