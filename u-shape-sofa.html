About the U-Shape Sofa

Every u-shape sofa we build is made to order in our Blackburn workshop. That means you choose the fabric, the colour and the exact dimensions — so if your alcove is 20cm narrower than a standard sofa, we build it 20cm narrower rather than asking you to compromise.

Frames are solid hardwood with deep foam and fibre cushioning, and every piece carries a manufacturer's guarantee. We deliver free across mainland UK, typically within 2–3 days of your order being confirmed, and cash on delivery is available so you can pay when it arrives.

Common questions

Can I have the U-Shape Sofa in a custom size?

Yes. Send us your room dimensions on WhatsApp and we'll adjust the length, depth and configuration to fit. There's no extra charge for standard resizing.

What fabrics can I choose from?

Plush velvet, Naple woven, Kensington premium weave, Chenille, Crush Velvet and Jumbo Cord — all hard-wearing and easy-clean. Message us for the full colour card.

How long does delivery take?

Made to order and delivered free across mainland UK within 2–3 days of confirming your order.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. You can pay a deposit and settle the balance on delivery, or choose full cash on delivery where available.
